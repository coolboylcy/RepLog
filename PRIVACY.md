# RepLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

RepLog is a strength training log app. We respect your privacy and keep the app simple.

## Data Collection

RepLog does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Workout records, exercise names, preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload your training data to our servers.

## Tracking

RepLog does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Analytics and Advertising

RepLog does not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs.

## Permissions

RepLog does not request access to location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone, HealthKit, or other sensitive device permissions.

## Data Retention

Because your data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app may delete the data stored by RepLog.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:

coolboylcy@hotmail.com

# RepLog 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 9 日

RepLog 是一款力量训练记录工具。我们尊重你的隐私，并尽量保持产品简单。

## 数据收集

RepLog 不收集、不传输、不出售、也不共享个人数据。

你的训练记录、动作名称、偏好设置和 App 设置均保存在你的设备本地。App 不需要账户，也不会把训练数据上传到我们的服务器。

## 跟踪

RepLog 不会跨其他公司拥有的 App 或网站跟踪你。

## 分析与广告

RepLog 不使用第三方分析 SDK 或广告 SDK。

## 权限

RepLog 不请求定位、通讯录、相机、照片、麦克风、HealthKit 或其他敏感设备权限。

## 数据保留

由于数据保存在你的设备本地，删除 App 可能会删除 RepLog 存储的数据。
