Promotional text:

Description:

RepLog is a clean strength training log for lifters who want to record workouts quickly and stay focused in the gym.

Log weight, reps, and sets during each session, review workout history, monitor training volume, and track personal records over time. RepLog supports both kg and lb, includes common muscle groups and exercises, and stores your data locally on your device.

Key features:

- Fast workout logging for weight, reps, and sets
- Workout history and session details
- Training insights including total sets, volume, recent activity, and personal records
- Custom exercises
- Rest timer
- kg and lb support
- English and Simplified Chinese interface
- Local-first storage with no account required
